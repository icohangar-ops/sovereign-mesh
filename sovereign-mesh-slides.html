Problem

Why agentic apps fail in production

Production AI systems usually break in the same three places: hidden retries, unsafe promotions, and missing evidence when operators need to audit the path.

Retry storms snowball into duplicate work

Timeout loops and flaky downstream calls can trigger double writes, queue buildup, and silent drift unless a runtime guard stops the cascade.

Unsafe promotions slip through

New tools, models, or workflows can reach production without the right policy check, turning a change request into an uncontrolled release.

Evidence disappears after the fact

Without structured traces and signed logs, teams cannot reconstruct what happened, who approved it, or why the runtime recovered the way it did.

SovereignMesh exists to add the missing runtime layer: control before execution, adversarial review for risk, and replayable evidence for every decision.
Solution

The control plane layers

Every high-stakes action moves through this stack before execution. The runtime stays small, typed, and easy to reason about while still enforcing policy and evidence.

1

Agent Registry

Declares agent roles, capabilities, and trusted scopes explicitly instead of assuming ambient access.

2

Zero-Trust ReBAC

Zanzibar-style viewer, editor, executor, and council auditor relations gate tool use and execution.

3

Model Armor

Rejects payloads that look like prompt injection, role hijacking, path traversal, or secret exfiltration.

4

CHP v1.0

Proposer, challenger, and adjudicator debate the action and compute a consensus confidence score.

5

Runtime Shield + Ledger

Tracks retries, breaker state, queue depth, and signed trace certificates for replayable evidence.

The goal is not to slow agents down. The goal is to make production AI behave like critical infrastructure: governed, observable, and recoverable.

What the user sees

  • Policy gate decisions
  • Consensus scores and challenger rebuttals
  • Runtime state, retries, and breaker transitions
  • Signed evidence artifacts for audit and replay

What the system guarantees

  • Fail closed when the policy or model armor says no
  • Lock high-stakes actions only above the confidence floor
  • Keep the execution path observable at every step
  • Make recovery explainable after the incident
Impact

What teams get

SovereignMesh turns agent execution from a best-effort experiment into a governed operational system with clear policy, resilience, and evidence.

Operational control

  • Agents act only inside explicit capabilities.
  • High-stakes requests are reviewed adversarially before they execute.
  • Retry storms and breaker trips are visible instead of hidden.

Auditability

  • Every decision produces replayable evidence.
  • Traces are signed and tied to a specific lock or rejection.
  • Operators can reconstruct why the runtime allowed or blocked an action.
